# Mount (planned)

This will hold the printable phone stand and SwitchBot Bot bracket (OpenSCAD source plus STL). A first rig has been built outside this repo.

The requirements:
- hold the phone rigidly, within the camera's minimum focus distance (about 8–10 cm or more). The camera may view the display at an angle, and the display may sit turned (Refine and `photoRotation`, ADRs 0013 and 0014);
- keep the display in darkness; the phone's torch is the only light (ADR 0014). Angle the light so its reflection doesn't wash out a digit;
- give the Bot's arm a straight press onto the membrane button.

See ADRs 0002, 0003, 0013 and 0014, and `TODO.md`.
